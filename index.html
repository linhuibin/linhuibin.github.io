👋About Me

I am a faculty member in the College of Computer and Information Sciences at Fujian Agriculture and Forestry University. My research focuses on building reliable learning systems that generalize beyond their training data, with particular interests in transfer learning, domain generalization, semi-supervised learning, and multimodal learning.

I received my Ph.D. from Fuzhou University, where my doctoral research was supervised by Prof. Chun-Yang Zhang. Before that, I received my master's degree from Fuzhou University, advised by Prof. Wenzhong Guo and Assoc. Prof. Zhanghui Liu.

Research interests: computer vision, domain generalization, transfer learning, semi-supervised learning, and multimodal representation learning.

🎓Prospective Students

Students interested in computer vision and machine learning are welcome to get in touch about research collaboration and potential student opportunities. I especially value curiosity, careful experimentation, clear communication, and the willingness to understand ideas beyond simply running code.
